## English

### Title

7 more Android testers needed — CUEE, genuine 14-day test exchange

### Body

Hi! I have 5 people lined up for CUEE's Google Play closed test and am looking for 7 more Android testers for a genuine 14-day testing exchange.

Join the tester group: https://groups.google.com/g/cuee-closed-test
Opt in on Google Play: [OPT_IN_URL]

CUEE is an Android accessibility guide for people with cognitive or learning difficulties who struggle with complex train-booking screens. It highlights the next place for the user to tap. It never automates login, booking, or payment, and it stops guidance before payment.

The app's interface and spoken guidance are in Korean. Korean-speaking testers with access to Korail+ can test the train-search guidance. Other testers can report installation, onboarding, text-input, permission-explanation, and layout issues; please mention which parts you could actually test. Current compatibility with real Korail+ is still being validated.

Exchange terms:

1. Stay continuously opted in to the closed test for 14 days.
2. Test the core flow at least three times: at the start, midway through, and near the end.
3. Send specific bug or usability feedback through private Play feedback or DM.
4. I will test your app under the same conditions and send private feedback.
5. This is not a public rating or review exchange.

No ticket purchase or payment is required when testing CUEE. Stop before any screen that displays personal or payment information.

If interested, please DM me your Android device/model, Android version, and closed-test link. We can confirm the schedule and exchange links before starting.
